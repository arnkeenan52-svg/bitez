what's inside

vegan pea protein for the numbers, pectin for the chew. no gelatin, nothing from animals. the full ingredient list goes on the printed label and on this page before launch. an independent lab in denmark tests every batch and we publish the reports.

shipping & pre-order

pre-orders ship in 8–12 weeks, EU first. nothing is charged today: when your order is ready, we email you a payment link. pay then, and it ships. change your mind? ignore the email and the order lapses.

what you're getting.

green apple bitez are translucent gummy bears with a sharp, sour apple bite. the base is pectin, the fruit fibre that sets classic wine gums, so the chew snaps clean. no gelatin at any step.

each big bag carries 20g of hydrolyzed pea protein. hydrolyzed means the protein is broken into fragments small enough to dissolve into the candy. you taste apple, not protein powder.

every production batch goes to an independent lab in denmark before it ships. protein, sugars and energy get measured, and the report gets published. the number on the bag is a measurement, not an estimate.

  • 20g protein per 96g bag, 10g per 48g, 30g per 144g
  • ~8g sugar per 96g bag, measured every batch
  • pectin base, 100% vegan, no gelatin
  • independent lab report published per batch
  • ships from denmark, EU first
  • pre-order: you pay only when it ships
