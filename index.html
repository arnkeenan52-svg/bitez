faq

fair questions.

is it really vegan?

yes. the protein is hydrolyzed pea protein and the gummy base is pectin, a fruit fibre. no gelatin, nothing from animals.

how much protein is in a bag?

10g in the small bag (48g), 20g in the big bag (96g), 30g in the share bag (144g). that's the tested number, not a marketing number.

how does pre-ordering work?

reserve your bags now with your email. nothing is charged today. when your order is ready to ship, in 8 to 12 weeks, we email you a payment link. you pay then, and it ships. pre-order here.

where do you ship?

we ship to the EU first. more countries after that.

why should i trust the label?

an independent lab tests every batch and we publish the reports. check the numbers yourself.
